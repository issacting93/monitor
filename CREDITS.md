# Credits and licences — TAPE 08

TAPE 08 is a USB-audio monitor, multitrack looper and effects rack for the teenage
engineering OP-XY.

The text below is the same text the app itself carries: the About panel (app menu ▸
**About TAPE 08**, and Help ▸ **Credits and Licences**, which opens the same panel)
shows it from inside the binary. That copy is the one that ships; this file is the
repository's record of it. Change one, change the other.

## Apple frameworks

EQ, reverb and delay are Apple's own audio units — `AVAudioUnitEQ`,
`AVAudioUnitReverb` and `AVAudioUnitDelay` — reached through `AVAudioEngine` and
`AVAudioUnitEffect`. They ship with the operating system and are used through their
public API.

## Everything else

The remaining kernels — passthrough, gain, distortion, chorus, compressor, flanger,
phaser, tremolo, bitcrusher and noise gate (`Sources/EffectsKernels.swift`) — are
original to TAPE 08, as is the rest of the application.

## Fonts and assets

No third-party fonts, samples or artwork. The app icon is generated by `package.sh`.
